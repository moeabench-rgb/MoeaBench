    reference

    The DTLZ6 benchmark problem is part of the list of proprietary implementations of the MoeaBench framework. All characteristics and definitions of the problem were based on the article DEB, K.; THIELE, L.; LAUMANNS, M.; ZITZLER, E. Scalable test problems for evolutionary multi-objective optimization. In: ABRAHAM, A.; JAIN, L.; GOLDBERG, R. (eds.). Evolutionary multi-objective optimisation. Berlin: Springer, 2005. p. 105-145. For more information, click the link below:
